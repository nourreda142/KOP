A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before your next cup.

Everything you need to know about this demo.

Can I place a real order?

This is a front-end café concept. The menu is illustrative and no orders or payments are processed.

Does a reservation reach the café?

No. The planner validates your details and saves the latest demo request in this browser only.

Will my favourites stay saved?

Yes, when browser storage is available. You can remove any saved drink using its heart button.

Does the site work on mobile?

The layouts, navigation and forms adapt to smaller screens and support keyboard navigation.
