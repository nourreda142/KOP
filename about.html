OUR CONCEPT

Good coffee.
Even better company.

KOPPEE brings the warmth of a neighbourhood café into a modern digital experience. This student project explores how a thoughtful website can help people discover a menu and plan a visit.

We kept the original coffee identity and gave it a calmer, more expressive direction: warm colours, generous space and useful interactions.
